Getting there

Port Aransas sits on a barrier island on the Texas Gulf Coast. It is reached by a short car ferry from Aransas Pass, or by a passenger-only water taxi from the same dock. The nearest airport is Corpus Christi International, about forty minutes away including the ferry; San Antonio is a further option if fares run better, at roughly three hours by car.

The ferry runs continuously and does not take reservations — on a busy weekend, budget an extra half hour either way.
