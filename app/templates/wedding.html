Coming to the island

We know we are asking you to travel a long way. Here is everything you need, so that the getting there is the easy part.

Where to stay

Palmilla Beach

The ceremony and the rest of the weekend are all inside Palmilla, so staying there puts you a short golf-cart ride from everything. There are rentals across the resort and houses along the beach either side of it. Please book early — February is busy on this coast.

Palmilla Beach

Fly into

Corpus Christi — CRP

The closest airport, and a small, easy one — about forty-five minutes from us by road. San Antonio has many more flights and is about a three-hour drive.

Airport site →

Rent a car

You will want one

All the usual rental companies are at the airport. Please book early — February is busy on this coast, and the island fills up.

Car rentals at CRP →

The last mile

The ferry is free

The island is reached by ferry from Aransas Pass. It runs day and night, takes under ten minutes, and does not take reservations.

Ferry information →

Getting around

You will not need the car again

Once you have landed, the island runs on golf carts and short walks. {{ event.location }} and the beach are close enough that the rental car can mostly stay parked.
