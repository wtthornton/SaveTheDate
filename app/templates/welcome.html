There is plenty of practical detail on the next page — schedule, times, what to bring for a beach in February. First we wanted to tell you why we are asking you to come all this way to a small island in Texas.

The short version: this island has been part of Lisa's family for decades, and we would like it to be part of ours.

Why Port Aransas

I

It began as a family vacation

Long before any of this, this was where Lisa's family came on vacation. Her parents loading up the car, the wait for the ferry, the same stretch of sand year after year. It was never just a trip — it was the place they came back to, and they have been coming back for as long as she can remember.

A Shore Thing

II

Then Jason built a place

Some years ago Lisa's brother Jason bought a piece of the island and built a house on it — A Shore Thing — and what had been a vacation turned into something steadier. Birthdays, holidays, long weekends for no particular reason — the family kept coming down, and kept bringing more people with them. It has been full ever since.

III

And now we would like you to see it

This is not a venue we found in a brochure. It is a place that has held this family for decades, and when we asked ourselves where we ought to marry, there was never really a second answer.

We would like to hand you the whole island for a few days — the beach, the ferry, the fire, the bad jokes at dinner — and then marry each other in front of you.

Thank you for coming so far. It means a great deal more than we can fit on a website.
